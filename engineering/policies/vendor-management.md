# Vendor and third-party management

**Owner:** CTO · **Review:** yearly

1. **Every service the platform depends on is in the
   [vendor register](../registers/vendors.md)**: what it is for, who owns
   the account, the plan and cost, what data it receives, and how to exit.
2. **Company-owned accounts only.** Each vendor account is registered to a
   company email (a Google Workspace group such as `tech-admin@`), not a
   personal address, and has at least two admins.
3. **New vendors** need a feature/change request with the *new third-party
   service* flag ticked. The CTO checks: data shared (DPS review if personal
   data), security (MFA, SSO, data location), cost, and lock-in.
4. **Monthly cost check.** Actual bills are recorded in the monthly CTO report
   and compared with the budget (programme §12.3).
5. **Leaving a vendor** follows the register's exit notes; keys are revoked
   and data deletion is confirmed in writing.
